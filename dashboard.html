Add your Graph API key and BOT details under Settings, then press Refresh now.
  • Deviation = (Uniswap rate − BOT rate) ÷ BOT rate, treating 1 THBT as 1 THB. Positive means the pool gives more THBT per token than the BOT rate.
  • Thresholds: red when the absolute deviation is more than 4%, yellow when it is more than 2%.
  • BOT rate is the Daily Average Exchange Rate mid rate, published once per business day. On weekends and holidays the most recent published rate is used, and its date is shown under the rate. USDT and USDC are both compared against USD.
  • Uniswap rate is each pool's current price from the Uniswap v4 subgraph, which updates with every swap. Each pair uses its current (migrated) pool.
  • Auto-refresh runs only while this page is open.
