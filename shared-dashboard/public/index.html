How these numbers work
  • Deviation = (Uniswap rate − Bank Rate) ÷ Bank Rate, treating 1 THBT as 1 THB. Positive means the pool gives more THBT per token than the Bank Rate.
  • Rebalance is the single swap in that pool that would move its rate to the Bank Rate right now, worded as the amount to enter in the swap's source field: Sell X THBT (for the token) when the pool rate is below the Bank Rate, Buy THBT with X token when it's above. It's worked out from the pool's current liquidity ranges and includes the pool's swap fee, if any, on the amount paid in. It's an estimate: other trades before yours, or a pool hook, would change it. Hover over a value for details.
  • Thresholds: red when the absolute deviation is more than 4%, yellow when it is more than 2%. The team's alert channels get a message when a pair goes over 2% or 4%, and when it comes back within 2% (checked every 15 minutes).
  • Bank Rate is the official Daily Average Exchange Rate mid rate, published once per business day. On weekends and holidays the most recent published rate is used, and its date is shown under the rate. USDT and USDC are both compared against USD.
  • Uniswap rate is each pool's current price from the Uniswap v4 subgraph. Prices are shared by everyone viewing this page and are at most a few minutes old.
  • Recent trades are the 10 latest swaps in each pair's current pool, newest first. Buy means the trader received THBT, Sell means they paid THBT. Price is the rate that trade got: THBT amount ÷ token amount. Wallet is the address that signed the transaction; click it to open it on BaseScan, or click the time to open the transaction.
  • Refresh now fetches the latest prices and trades straight away, then pauses for 30 seconds. Auto-refresh uses the copy shared by everyone (at most 3 minutes old). A new trade can take from a few seconds to a minute or so to reach the subgraph.
