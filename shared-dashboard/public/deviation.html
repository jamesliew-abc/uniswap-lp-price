  • Deviation = (Uniswap price − Bank Rate) ÷ Bank Rate, as on the Live rates page. Positive means the pool gave more THBT per token than the Bank Rate.
  • Bank Rate is the Bank of Thailand's daily average mid rate, published around 18:00 Bangkok time on business days. Each capture uses the latest rate already published at that moment: the previous business day's rate at 09:00 and 15:00, and that day's rate at 21:00 and 03:00. USDT and USDC are compared against USD.
  • A gap means the pool hadn't traded yet, or no Bank Rate was available.
  • History is kept for the last 90 days. Newly captured prices are also posted to the team's alert channels.
